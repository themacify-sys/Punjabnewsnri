About PUNJAB NEWS NRI

Connecting global Punjabi communities through authentic journalism, media operations, and dedicated regional reporting.

Our Mission & Overview

PUNJAB NEWS NRI is an established media publication and digital information portal dedicated to delivering news, economic updates, and cultural stories connecting Punjab to the non-resident Indian (NRI) diaspora globally. Operating out of Ludhiana, Punjab, we maintain high standards of editorial integrity, transparency, and regional representation.

As a recognized enterprise under the Ministry of Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises (Govt. of India), our operations cover news distribution, media promotion, and advertising services targeted at localized and international audiences.
